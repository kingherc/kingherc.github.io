I had the pleasure of speaking at a recent meetup hosted by [DevStaff.gr](https://www.devstaff.gr), an open developers community based in Crete. As a member of their organizing team, I've been involved with these monthly free meetups that bring together tech enthusiasts from across the local developer scene.

## Meetup

The [meetup](https://www.meetup.com/devstaff/events/313173689/) was titled "Unlocking Tech Careers: Insider Insights & Job Hunting Strategies" and took place on February 12th, 2026. It was designed specifically for tech enthusiasts eager to enhance their career prospects in the software development field. The event featured two informative presentations.

## My Presentation

I had the pleasure of presenting "Software developer job hunting tips" at the meetup. It was a fantastic session filled with great discussions about navigating the modern tech job market! In this talk, I shared practical advice and strategies for software developers looking to land their next role in the competitive tech industry. We covered everything from optimizing your online presence to mastering technical interviews and understanding company culture.
