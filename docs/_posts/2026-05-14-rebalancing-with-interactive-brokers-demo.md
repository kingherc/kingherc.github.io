I had the pleasure of speaking at a recent meetup hosted by [DevStaff.gr](https://www.devstaff.gr), an open developers community based in Crete. As a member of their organizing team, I've been involved with these monthly free meetups that bring together tech enthusiasts from across the local developer scene.

## Meetup

The [meetup](https://www.meetup.com/devstaff/events/314693234/) was titled "Investments for Geeks" and took place on May 14th, 2026. It was designed as a fantastic gathering for fintech enthusiasts and anyone curious about smarter money management. The meetup provided an excellent platform to discuss modern personal finance tools and practical portfolio management strategies.

## My Presentation

I was delighted to present "Rebalancing with Interactive Brokers demo" at the meetup. I walked through practical methods for maintaining optimal portfolio allocation using Interactive Brokers, a powerful tool for investors. With screenshots and practical tips. And a screenshot of my current portfolio and percentages.
